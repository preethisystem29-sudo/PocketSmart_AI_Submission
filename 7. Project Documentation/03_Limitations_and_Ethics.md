# Limitations, Safety and Ethics

PocketSmart generates recommendations, not guaranteed financial outcomes.

Users should verify:
- Current prices
- Product availability
- Vendor reliability
- Taxes/shipping
- Event or travel constraints
- Any financial decision with an appropriate professional when necessary

The AI should not invent live prices or availability. The prototype prompt explicitly asks it to label estimates and avoid unsupported claims.

Privacy:
The prototype stores settings/history in the local browser. Do not enter sensitive financial credentials or publish personal financial data.
