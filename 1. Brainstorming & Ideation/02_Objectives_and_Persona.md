# Objectives and User Persona

## Objectives
- Provide budget-aware recommendations.
- Use Gemini generative AI for personalized suggestions.
- Make the interaction simple enough for first-time users.
- Keep recommendations within the stated budget.
- Allow users to save and revisit previous recommendations.
- Demonstrate a complete AI project lifecycle.

## Primary Persona
**Name:** Student Budget Planner

**Needs**
- Has a limited budget.
- Wants practical choices rather than generic suggestions.
- Needs a simple explanation of why a recommendation fits.

**Pain Points**
- Too many choices.
- Difficulty estimating how to distribute money.
- Risk of overspending.
- Difficulty comparing alternatives.

## Success Indicators
- User can generate a recommendation in a few steps.
- Suggested allocation does not exceed the stated budget.
- User can save and reopen a recommendation.
- AI response clearly explains assumptions and trade-offs.
